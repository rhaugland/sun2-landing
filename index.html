What it is (and isn't)

Not a therapist. Not HR. Something new.

Sun² fills the gap between "I'm fine" and "I need help" — the everyday moments that never make it to an EAP. The frustrating meeting. The email that stung. The Sunday dread.

When someone needs more than Sun² can offer, it points them to your EAP, benefits, and crisis resources.
